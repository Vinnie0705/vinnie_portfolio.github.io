A centralized digital platform designed to simplify the construction procurement process by connecting clients with contractors through an online project bidding system.

01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bringing construction procurement online.

The Online Construction Project Bidding Platform was developed as my Final Year Project to improve the traditional construction procurement process.

Instead of relying heavily on manual communication, document sharing and physical processes, the platform provides a centralized environment where clients can post projects and contractors can discover and submit bids.

The system also supports package purchasing, payment management, notifications and administrative management.

🏗️

Project Goal

To create a more organized, transparent and efficient platform for managing construction projects and contractor bidding activities.

02 — HOW IT WORKS

From project posting to bidding.

The platform organizes the construction procurement process into a simple digital workflow.

01

Client Posts Project

Clients create and publish construction projects with relevant project details and requirements.

02

Contractors Discover Projects

Contractors browse available projects and review project requirements before submitting a bid.

03

Contractor Submits Bid

Contractors submit their proposals and bidding information through the platform.

04

Client Reviews Bids

Clients can review submitted bids and select suitable contractors for their projects.
